# About ALTA Academy
ALTA Academy (Ալտա Ակադեմիա, Альта Академия) is an online education center in Armenia, run by individual entrepreneur Lianna Galstyan. All lessons are held online, so students can join from anywhere. Founder Lianna Galstyan has taught web development since 2016 and has 10 years of software development experience.

# Contacts

Registration is done through the registration form on the website. Applications are reviewed and the applicant receives an email answer (approved or not approved).

# Course formats
Courses are offered in two formats: group lessons and individual (one-to-one) lessons.
Individual lessons cost 125,000 AMD.

# Programming courses
Frontend web development course: online, 50,000 AMD per month.
Backend web development course: online, 50,000 AMD per month.
Frontend and backend are two separate courses; a student can take one or both to become a full-stack developer.

# AI courses
AI for everyone: 65,000 AMD per month.
AI for business and marketing: 95,000 AMD per month.
AI for developers: 70,000 AMD per month.

# Payment
Payment can be made online with Idram or with a bank card.
Students see their payment history on their personal page, and receive a reminder 5 days before each payment is due.

# Student account
After registration each student gets a personal account on the website. In the account the student can: read and electronically sign their own full contract, see their payment history, and watch the video lessons.
Only a sample contract is shown publicly on the website.

# Certificate
Graduates receive a certificate. A sample of the certificate is shown on the website.

# What the assistant does not know
Exact start dates, class schedules, course duration, discounts and installment plans are not published here. For these questions the assistant must connect the client with a staff member instead of guessing.
